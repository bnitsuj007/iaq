The science behind our testing +

Controlled test environment

We tested every configuration in the same enclosed space, using particle sensors and consistent furnace-fan operation so each setup could be compared on a level playing field.

CADR / PM2.5 / PM10

We tracked Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR) along with PM2.5 and PM10 particle levels. CADR combines airflow and particle removal into one number. The higher the result, the more clean air the system is effectively delivering.

ΔP / static pressure / airflow

We also measured pressure drop (ΔP) in in. w.c. at roughly 1,000 CFM. That let us compare particle-removal performance with how much resistance each setup added to the HVAC system.

Standard installations generally take 30 minutes to 3 hours. Additional ductwork changes can take longer. We will confirm the expected time with your options.

In most cases, yes. Compatibility matters more than a one-size-fits-all recommendation, which is exactly why we start with a photo of your equipment.
